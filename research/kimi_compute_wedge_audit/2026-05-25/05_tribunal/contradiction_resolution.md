# Commercialization Contradiction — Resolved

## The contradiction
Kimi states both:
- "Single best first paid wedge: GPU Equipment Lenders / Asset-Based Lenders."
- "Fastest path to first revenue runs through ITAD remarketing pilots."

These read as conflicting because they conflate three different questions.

## Resolution: separate the three questions
| question | answer | evidence basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fastest to **validate**? | Neocloud / GPU host | DIRECT_EVIDENCE (smash + Vast) — we already have a working run |
| Fastest to **first paid revenue**? | ITAD remarketing pilot | HONEY (6 verified ITADs) + JELLY (GPU fit, WTP) |
| Highest **long-term value**? | GPU lenders / ABL / insurers | JELLY/PROPOLIS today — unverified; do not lead here |

The "single best first paid wedge = lenders" statement is **not supported**: lender claims are JELLY/PROPOLIS and the sales cycle is longest. The "fastest revenue = ITAD" statement is the **defensible** near-term paid lane. Lenders are the *destination*, not the *entry*.

## Staged sequence (the disciplined position)
- **Stage 0 — Internal validation (now):** Swarm/Vast GPUs, smash receipt. DIRECT_EVIDENCE.
- **Stage 1 — External validation (fastest):** neocloud / GPU host pilots. Prove usefulness; gather WTP signal.
- **Stage 2 — First paid wedge:** ITAD / remarketing pilot. Condition/identity evidence at intake.
- **Stage 3 — Scale destination:** lenders / ABL / insurers, only after receipt quality + comp methodology + references are proven and lender requirements are primary-verified.

## Bottom line
Kimi's instinct (ITAD fast, lenders big) is directionally right. The error is calling lenders the "first paid wedge." Corrected order: **validate on neoclouds → earn first dollars on ITAD → scale into lenders later.**
