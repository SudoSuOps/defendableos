## The critical discipline finding

**Zero of the captured ITAD homepages mention "GPU" or "NVIDIA."** They advertise datacenter decommissioning, server lifecycle, remarketing, and data destruction. GPU handling is therefore **adjacent / inferred**, not an advertised capability.

> A company advertising datacenter decommissioning is a verified *relevance signal*, not proof it remarkets GPUs or will buy a Proof Receipt. The GPU-specific layer must be confirmed per-target before any GPU pitch.

## R2v3 reality check
R2v3 (SERI Responsible Recycling) governs **data sanitization, downstream accountability, and EH&S** — it does **not** create a market-*value* verification budget. The Kimi inference that "R2v3 creates a direct market-value verification budget" is **not supported**; R2v3 budgets fund data-destruction and compliance, not condition/comp receipts. Treat as JELLY.

## What is safe to act on now
- Prepare (not send) outreach research dossiers for the 6 verified ITADs, framed as **condition/identity evidence at decommission/remarketing intake** — never as appraisal or value lift.
- Drop Slyd/STS/Maxicom from the target list until a resolving official domain is found.
- For each verified ITAD, the open hypothesis is identical: *do they handle GPU-dense nodes, and would a benchmark-backed condition record lift remarketing outcomes?* That is a pilot question, not a settled fact.
