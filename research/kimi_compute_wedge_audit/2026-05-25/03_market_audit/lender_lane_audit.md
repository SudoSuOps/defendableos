# Lender / ABL Lane Audit

## Verdict
The lender lane is the **highest potential long-term value** but the **least source-verified** lane in the package. Every dollar-specific lending claim remains **JELLY**, and one (Munich Re) is **PROPOLIS**. This lane must not appear in any external material yet.

## Barkr positioning
Barkr is a **pricing/valuation** service ("AI-powered, insurance-backed valuations for hard assets … help lenders reduce risk"). DefendableOS captures **hardware condition/identity evidence**. These are complementary layers. The disciplined position is **partner-candidate, not competitor** — but no partnership, endorsement, or Munich Re linkage may be stated externally.

## Hard prohibition for this lane
Until primary filings/covenants are located, DefendableOS must **never** state: "accepted by lenders," "satisfies OCC collateral appraisal requirements," "cuts financing costs $X," "lender-approved valuation," or "insurance-backed." (The product doctrine already bans these terms — this audit confirms the ban.)
