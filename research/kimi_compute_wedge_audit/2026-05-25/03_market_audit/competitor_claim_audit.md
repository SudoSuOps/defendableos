# Competitor Claim Audit

## Verdict
The "**49 competitors scanned · no competitor combines all five layers**" claim is **PROPOLIS as worded** — an unfalsifiable absolute over an unspecified set. It must be rewritten before any use, and kept out of federal/marketing entirely.

## Approved rewrite
> "The audited sample did not reveal a single platform publicly describing all identified layers (market comps + benchmark evidence + tamper-evident receipt + sale package + transferable deed) in one offering. Further competitive verification is required."

## Verified competitor/adjacent platforms (existence = HONEY; overlap analysis)
| platform | what it actually is (from its own page) | overlap with DefendableOS | competitor or partner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blancco | Data erasure software for enterprises/ITAD/mobile | **Data-destruction layer only** — no benchmark/comps/deed | adjacent / possible complement |
| Spherity | Digital identity solutions (DPP-aligned) | **Identity/credential & DPP layer** — no compute benchmark/comps | adjacent / possible complement |
| EquipmentWatch | Equipment price data, serial search, APIs | **Comps/appraisal-data** — but heavy-equipment vertical, not GPUs | adjacent (different asset class) |
| Barkr | AI, insurance-backed asset valuation for lenders | **Pricing/valuation layer** — no hardware benchmark evidence | partner-candidate (see lender audit) |
| Vast.ai | GPU rental marketplace w/ host verification | **Runtime host verification** — no comps/deed/standardized grade | partner-candidate / channel |

## Finding
Each verified platform occupies **one** layer. None publicly advertises the full comps+benchmark+receipt+sale+deed stack — which *supports the differentiation thesis* but only as a sampled observation, not an absolute. The differentiation is real enough to build on; the "no competitor" absolute is not safe to assert.

## Rule
- Internal/build: differentiation thesis is usable (HONEY-adjacent reasoning).
- External/federal/marketing: only the softened, sampled wording above; never "the only platform" or "no competitor."
