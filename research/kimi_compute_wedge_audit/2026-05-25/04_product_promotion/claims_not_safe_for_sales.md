# Claims NOT Safe for Sales / External Use (v0.1)

These are PROHIBITED until separately supported by documented primary evidence. Each maps to an audited claim.

| prohibited claim | why | source claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Cuts lender financing costs by $4M per facility" | single-analyst estimate; product ROI unproven | KCW-CLAIM-0011 |
| "Increases H100 resale price by 25%" | secondary pricing; no causal proof | KCW-CLAIM-0016 |
| "Accepted by lenders" / "lender-approved valuation" | no lender acceptance evidence | KCW-CLAIM-0010/0013 |
| "Backed by Munich Re" | not on Barkr's own site; not our relationship anyway | KCW-CLAIM-0012 |
| "Satisfies OCC collateral appraisal requirements" | no primary OCC GPU requirement located | KCW-CLAIM-0013 |
| "Complies with HIPAA" | HIPAA changes are proposed (NPRM), not final | KCW-CLAIM-0020 |
| "Complies with EU Digital Product Passport" | ESPR/DPP per-product timeline unconfirmed for ICT | KCW-CLAIM-0019 |
| "Verifies export eligibility" | can record identity, not determine ECCN eligibility | KCW-CLAIM-0021 |
| "Government approved" / "Federal certified" | SAM only submitted; no award/approval | federal boundary |
| "Appraisal-grade" / "Insurance approved" | banned in product doctrine; no accreditation | doctrine |
| "Only platform combining all five layers" / "no competitor" | unfalsifiable absolute | KCW-CLAIM-0022 |
| "Validated pricing index" | comps are secondary; no index exists | KCW-CLAIM-0016 |
| Any market-size figure stated as fact ($20B, $11.7B, $12.95B→$19.94B) | secondary/projection only | KCW-CLAIM-0010/0014/0015 |
| Slyd / STS / Maxicom named as customers/targets | identity unverifiable | KCW-CLAIM-0023 |

Rule: if a claim is about what a *third party* (lender, insurer, regulator, government, market) will do or accept, it is not sales-safe in v0.1.
