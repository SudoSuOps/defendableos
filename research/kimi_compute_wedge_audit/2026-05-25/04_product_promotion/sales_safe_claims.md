# Sales-Safe Claims (v0.1)

These statements are supported by DIRECT_EVIDENCE or verified workflow evidence and stay within product doctrine. Safe for sales conversations and decks.

## Approved language
- "Defendable Compute Proof Receipt captures benchmark-backed condition evidence for high-value AI compute."
- "The receipt documents an asset's observed identity, runtime state, benchmark results, operating baseline, and artifact hashes at a specific timestamp."
- "It distinguishes first-party residue from tenant contamination — on a real post-rental cycle it surfaced 28 GB of held VRAM and traced it to an internal service, not the renter."
- "The asset passed a remote validation suite (system requirements, ResNet18, ECC, NCCL, simultaneous stress + GPU burn) and the receipt records that pass/fail evidence."
- "Designed for rent-ready, resale-ready, disposition-intake, and fleet-audit workflows."
- "Every receipt is hash-anchored and independently verifiable against its named evidence artifacts."
- "Market comps and value opinions are future layers, subject to evidence-quality and methodology controls."

## Framing rules
- Always timestamp-scoped ("at the time of observation").
- Always condition/evidence language, never value/appraisal language.
- Claims about *what the receipt records* are safe; claims about *what a third party will accept* are not.
