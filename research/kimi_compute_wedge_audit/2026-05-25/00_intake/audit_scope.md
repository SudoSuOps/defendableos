## Tribunal class rules in effect

- **DIRECT_EVIDENCE** — observed Swarm operational telemetry (e.g., the smash RTX 5090 post-renter sequence). The strongest tier.
- **HONEY** — externally sourced and the cited source has been located and confirmed to support the exact claim. Safe for internal build decisions and supporting research; requires claim-boundary review before public marketing.
- **JELLY** — plausible but unverified, single-source, proposed-not-final, or contingent on customer behavior. Useful for hypothesis testing; never sales-safe without further evidence.
- **PROPOLIS** — unsupported, contradicted, legally unsafe, inflated, fabricated-looking, or reputationally dangerous. Excluded from all external materials.
