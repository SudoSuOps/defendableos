REFUND POLICY · effective 2026-01-01

1. Damaged-on-arrival items qualify for full refund within 30 days
   of delivery. Customer must provide photo evidence.
2. Unwanted items qualify for full refund within 14 days of
   delivery · item must be unused and in original packaging.
3. Refunds above $250 require operator approval.
4. Refunds to a different account/email than the order require
   operator approval.
5. Agent may DRAFT refund decisions only · live issuance requires
   operator confirmation through the admin panel.
