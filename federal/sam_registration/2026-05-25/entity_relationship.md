```
Federal customer (e.g., DOT, NRC, DoD)
              │
              ▼ contract signed with ↓
       CABALLERZ NETWORK LLC
              │
              ▼ executes work under ↓
         DefendableOS / DCPR
              │
              ▼ deliverables built on ↓
   Swarm and Bee LLC infrastructure

```

Caballerz Network LLC is the named contract holder. Swarm and Bee LLC is the underlying compute and dataset provider operating as a subcontractor / supplier inside the Caballerz contract scope. Both are operated by the same founder; contractually they are distinct.

## Why the separation matters

- **Brokerage isolation:** Swarm and Bee LLC holds a Florida real estate brokerage license (CRE vertical). Federal IT/software contracting under that LLC would unnecessarily entangle the brokerage regulatory perimeter. Caballerz Network LLC keeps the federal lane clean of CRE-broker compliance overhead.
- **Risk isolation:** Federal contracts carry distinct insurance, indemnification, and liability profiles. Holding them under a separate LLC limits exposure.
- **Clean books and records:** Each LLC keeps its own books · receivables · audit trail. Tribunal-grade discipline applies independently to each.
- **Different banking rails** can be applied per LLC if a federal customer requires a specific banking arrangement.
