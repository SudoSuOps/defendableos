# Outbound Claim Boundaries (product market_validation)

## May say (DIRECT_EVIDENCE / reviewed HONEY)
- What the receipt records: identity, runtime state, benchmark pass/fail, baseline, hashes — timestamp-scoped.
- That it surfaced and traced held VRAM on a real post-rental cycle.
- That an asset passed a named remote validation suite.
- That it is designed for rent-ready / resale-ready / disposition-intake / fleet-audit workflows.

## May NOT say (JELLY / PROPOLIS)
- Any market-size or pricing figure as fact ($20B, $11.7B, $12.95B→$19.94B, 15–25%, +40%, $50–150).
- "Cuts financing costs $4M" / "increases resale 25%" / "accepted by lenders" / "lender-approved."
- "Backed by Munich Re" / "insurance-backed" (Barkr's wording, not ours).
- "Satisfies OCC / HIPAA / EU DPP" / "verifies export eligibility" / "compliant."
- "Government approved" / "federal certified" / "appraisal-grade" / "validated index."
- "Only platform / no competitor combines all five layers."
- Slyd / STS / Maxicom as customers or targets.

Rule: claims about what a third party (lender/insurer/regulator/government/market) will accept are out of bounds in v0.1. Mirrors `doctrine/terminology_and_claim_boundaries.md`.
